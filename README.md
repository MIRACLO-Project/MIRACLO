# MIRACLO

*Pure Android 17, made to feel like yours.*

**MIRACLO 1.0 "Aurora"** is an Android 17 ROM built on Qualcomm's own Android source (CLO) for the
**POCO F5 (marble)**. It stays close to clean Android: Google's design, Google's apps, no
bloat. On top it adds **Mira Studio**, one place where you choose how the phone looks, moves and
behaves. Every addition is off until you turn it on.

> **Status:** MIRACLO is in active development. The first official release is being prepared;
> follow [@MIRACLO_Project](https://t.me/MIRACLO_Project) to hear when it is out.

## Mira Studio

Settings › **Mira Studio**. Everything MIRACLO adds, in one place, grouped as cards.

### Panels
- **Quick Settings styles**: ColorOS, iOS and One UI layouts, or Android's own. They are built on
  Android's real tiles, so every tile, its long-press and its details work as usual.
- **Edit mode in every style**: resize tiles (ColorOS Round, Pill and Large; iOS 1×1, 2×1, 1×2
  and 2×2; One UI Small and Large), hold and drag to move, add any tile Android has, and move or
  hide widgets (media, sliders, connectivity).
- **iOS style extras**: a connectivity box you fill yourself (up to six controls), a full
  connectivity card, and Now Playing with a wavy seek bar you can drag.
- **One UI style**: drag anywhere on the tile area to open or close it.
- **Volume panel** and **Liquid Glass** for Quick Settings, notifications and the volume panel.
- Quick Settings opens like the notification shade; in landscape, notifications sit on the
  left and Quick Settings on the right.

### Top bar
- **Icon styles**: Android 17, ColorOS, iOS or One UI for Wi-Fi, signal and battery.
- **Dynamic Island** for calls, media, timers, charging and more.
- Network speed, network type label, stacked SIMs, VoLTE and VoWiFi icons, clock seconds, and
  which status icons show.
- **Lock screen clocks** in several styles.

### Home
- Icon shapes (circle by default), icon packs and themed icons, for the home screen and the app
  drawer separately.
- Gestures: double-tap to lock, swipe down for search, notifications or Quick Settings, swipe
  up, pinch.
- Page transitions, labels on or off, layout lock, folder blur, hidden apps, home search,
  recents styles.

### Style
- **Colours** from your wallpaper or a colour you pick, in every Material You style.
- **Wallpapers**, **fonts**, and **Animations**: Standard, Expressive or Snappy motion, with
  four speeds, across the whole system.
- Material 3 Expressive **wavy progress bars and spinners** everywhere Android draws its own.

### Tuning
- Display and battery tuning.
- **Power menu**: Emergency, Power off, Restart and Advanced (Restart System UI, reboot to
  recovery, reboot to bootloader).

Install guides come with the first release: clean install from HyperOS, updates between

### Bringing MIRACLO to a device

Maintainers who want to build MIRACLO for another device can request access to the Mira Studio
repositories (below). Builds for other devices need a working Android 17 device tree.
